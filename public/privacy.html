Privacy Policy — Did I Lock?

Last updated: 13 October 2026 · Applies to this website and its installable app (PWA)

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address (and phone number if you sign in by phone). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • Your content: your checklist items, tasks, and departure check-in history.
  • Home zone: if you save it, your home coordinates and alert radius. This exists only so the app can tell when you have left home. It is never shown to other users, never placed in URLs, and never sold or shared.
  • Notifications: if you enable push, your browser's push subscription is stored so we can send you the check-in reminder.

What we do NOT do

  • No analytics, no advertising, no trackers, no profiling.
  • We never sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

Third parties

  • Hosting: the app runs on Render (US/EU/Singapore data centres).
  • Google: if you sign in with Google, Google handles the authentication. We receive only your Google account id, name, and a verified email.
  • Twilio: if phone login is enabled on this deployment, Twilio delivers SMS codes. We send only the code.

Storage & retention

Data is stored in a database on the app's server. Sessions use tokens that expire after 7 days. Guest-mode data ("Try it first") never leaves your device — it lives in your browser's local storage and is removed when you clear it or merge it into an account.

Deleting your data

Open the app → Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your account, checklist, history, home zone, tasks, and push subscriptions. You can also email the operator of this deployment for the same effect.

Your rights (India DPDP Act, 2023)

You may request access, correction, or erasure of your personal data, and withdraw consent (deleting your account) at any time. The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: the operator of this deployment (see the app's listing or contact page).

Email delivery: when you use email sign-in or email password reset, the message is delivered by our email provider, Resend (Resend Inc.), which receives your email address and the message content solely to deliver it. Verification and reset links expire automatically.
